Bounded unit-return scoring: one coherent return-representation/scoring change, not a feature stack.

C is the declared number of OGBench reward components (navigation1,CubeD2,Pz3=9,Pz4=16,Scene5), not tuned from performance or observedminimum15forPz4. With a=(1-gamma)/C, p_Q=1+a*Q and p_V=1+a*V. Known cost range[-C/(1-gamma),0] becomes[0,1]. The transformed reward is a*r+1-gamma*mask, implemented stably as (1-gamma)*(1+r/C)+gamma*(1-mask). Thus y_p=transformed_reward+gamma*mask*p_V_next =1+a*(r+gamma*mask*V_next). Positive-affine expectiles and action order are preserved. Manipulation shaped rewards are NOT replaced by binary successes; normalized returns are not generally pure success probabilities.

Official four-layer GELU bodies, layer normalization, two V Adam fits, freshV bootstrap, old-online-Q EMA, one critic/BC update, lr.0003, tau.9,gamma.999,N9, and original unweightedBCflow remain common. Raw critic/V outputs are logits, decoded by sigmoid to[0,1]. Deployment best9 min raw online logits preserves monotone decoded-Q order without sigmoid rounding ties. No auxiliary policy, ensemble, finite-action bootstrap or action gradients throughQ.

Compare2newgloballyfixed profiles onAnt/HMM/Pz3 seed3: (1)unit_MSE uses asymmetric probability MSE forV and probability MSE forQ; (2)unit_KL uses asymmetric Bernoulli KL(qtarget||V) forV and soft-target Bernoulli cross-entropy forQ. KL subtracts targetentropy, making the asymmetric loss continuous whenqtarget=V. Its logit derivative is w_tau*(V-qtarget); therefore its population root is the original expectile, while probability-MSE has an additional2V(1-V)factor. The conditional critic BCE root is E[y_p]. Neural convergence/performance is not guaranteed. Endpoint roots lie in the closure; finite sigmoid logits approximate them.
