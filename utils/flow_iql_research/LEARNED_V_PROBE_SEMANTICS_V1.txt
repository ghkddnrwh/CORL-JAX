Additional clarification: generic learned-V probe semantics
===========================================================

The earlier DIAGNOSTIC_TARGET_SEMANTICS.txt focuses on finite-backup branches,
where the generic learned-V td_rms also uses the WRONG bootstrap variable for
their actual Q update. A separate qualification applies even to ordinary
bounded_v2 IQL/IFQL, including the preferred three-critic policy's base learner.

core.probe uses current parameters on fixed replay queries:
  y_raw = r + gamma * mask * V_current(s_next)
  td_rms = RMS(Q_current - y_raw).
It does not project y_raw. The bounded_v2 learner actually projects its fresh-V
training target onto[L,0]. Consequently the generic td_rms is an UNPROJECTED
snapshot Bellman-difference proxy, not its actual projected fitting residual.
The raw-y difference can differ from the projected snapshot residual whenever
the target clips. Even the projected snapshot residual is not the old online
training minibatch's residual, because its queries and parameter time differ.

In core.build_update and its unchanged policy_core/AWR-mixture delegates,
critic/critic_loss is the ACTUAL two-head sum of projected squared errors.
For that loss alone, sqrt(critic_loss/2) recovers the RMS of the two heads.
When logged as an average over an update window, it is a window RMS over the
changing parameters/replay minibatches, not the fixed-query snapshot quantity.
The formula does not automatically apply to auxiliary-Q-loss branches or to
other agents' differently normalized critic loss definitions. Check each source.

Likewise score_rms is RMS over individual fixed sampled(state,data_action)
expectile scores. It is not RMS of the conditional mean score given the state.
score_mean can cancel between states; neither statistic identifies the true
conditional expectile residual or propagated B_k without additional assumptions.
In finite-backup branches the learned V can additionally be an unused auxiliary
head, so its score need not diagnose the bootstrap that trains Q at all.

All training equations, checkpoints, evaluation successes, and the preferred
discovery9-task last3 mean.5459259259259259 are unchanged. The correction concerns
the interpretation of logged probes. No pinned source/log was edited. Read this
qualification together with DIAGNOSTIC_TARGET_SEMANTICS.txt; its narrower
'other finite branches only' wording does not make bounded_v2 td_rms a projected
training-error metric. External learned-V snapshots explicitly report both.
