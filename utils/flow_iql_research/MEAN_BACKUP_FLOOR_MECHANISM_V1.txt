on9behavior-flow actions and project Bellman targets to known cost bounds.
Nine-task seed0 success is .4000 for min heads and .617037 for mean heads.
Actual54late checkpoints have identical BC weights/moments/replay/agent RNG.

This is evidence for a target-aggregation effect, not environment-wise tuning.

Why tiny finite residual is insufficient
The solver score is around numerical precision for either aggregation. That
only certifies an expectile of the9sampled learned-Q values. A systematically
shifted or poorly ranked critic can have an exact empirical root. It is not
a statement that population-behavior residual, true-Q error or propagated B_k
is zero. Bounded target values can also coexist with a useless saturated
critic, so numerical finiteness is not a sufficient definition of stability.

Mathematical mechanism and its assumptions
For two numbers, mean(Q1,Q2)-min(Q1,Q2)=abs(Q1-Q2)/2 pointwise. If two scalar
errors have zero conditional means, taking their minimum introduces a negative
expected error of half their expected absolute difference; the arithmetic
mean removes that explicit minimum. Learned heads need not be unbiased, so
their observed gap is not a measured true-Q bias.
Expectiles are monotone and translation-equivariant. Under a FIXED teacher,
the mean-head empirical root is therefore at least its minimum-head root,
apart from floating-point effects. This does not order independently trained
critics or prove that a more optimistic root is closer to true action value.
In an ideal unprojected scalar fixed operator, a constant per-backup shift can
accumulate geometrically. Multiplying an observed mean root gap by1/(1-gamma)
does not estimate B_k for these changing nonlinear neural networks. Projection
can saturate at the lower bound; it does not guarantee useful action ranking.

Logging distinctions
Clipping/loss/root metrics are averages of actual1000-update minibatch windows.
Fixed-query Q/V statistics are instantaneous on1024sampled states; their time
means are descriptive and are not full-replay population averages.
The learned V retained in BOTH finite implementations is a min-teacher clock
control and never enters the bootstrap. root_mean - V_min combines a head
aggregation effect, density/finite-sampling effects and V fitting; it is not
a pure residual measurement. Subtracting mean root_mean-root_min from mean
root_mean-V_min gives a mean root_min-V_min discrepancy, but RMS quantities
cannot be subtracted this way. No teacher is a nonterminal Q oracle here.
