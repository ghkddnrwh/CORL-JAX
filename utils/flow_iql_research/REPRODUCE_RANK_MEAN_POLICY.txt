Current preferred candidate: IQLV2 + BC flow + mean critic ranks
==============================================================

The algorithm was chosen from the complete four-rule DISCOVERY comparison on
existing learners0/1/2. Its nine-task last3 mean is.5459259259259259. Fresh
confirmation is pending. The older median-rank candidate and its registration
are preserved as a paired comparator; no historical results were overwritten.

Training is the already registered IFQLV2 recipe, three independent learners:
  freeze old target-minQ on a minibatch and take2 V Adam fits;
  bootstrap Q with fresh V(s_next), projecting r+gamma*mask*V onto[L,0];
  take one Q and one unweighted BC flow-matching update, preserving official
  target-EMA ordering. Each learner has independent initialization/replay RNG.
There is no Gaussian AWR actor, policy-flow student, finite expectile bootstrap,
boundary auxiliary loss or temporal K3 component in this candidate.

Deploy ONLY the first learner's BC flow. Generate9 action proposals using10
Euler steps. Each of3 learners evaluates them with its own minimum twin-Q.
Convert that learner's9 values to midranks, average the3 rank vectors, and
choose the highest average. Ties choose the first IID proposal. All9 tasks use
tau.9,gamma999,N9,3 learners and the same rule. Widths and other base settings
follow the pinned official-style configuration by environment family.

Run from the repository root with the ogbench conda environment. For each
environment train learners3,4,5 (and then6,7,8 for the second fresh group):

  CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=<assigned GPU> XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_MEM_FRACTION=0.1 \
  OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 MUJOCO_GL=egl \
  python -m utils.flow_iql_research.train_policy_v2 \
    --env <OGBench environment> --output <member directory> \
    --kind ifql --profile bounded_v2 --seed <member seed> \
    --discount .999 --expectile .9 --samples 9 \
    --steps 1000000 --episodes 50 --aux-eval-steps ''
