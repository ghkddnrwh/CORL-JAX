Research mechanism and comparison map
====================================

This is a map of alternative experiments and their interpretation, not a recipe
that stacks every available technique. No uniform nine-task method has yet
passed independent confirmation above the supplied plain FQL threshold .47.
The supplied plain ReBRAC threshold is .44; DD-ReBRAC .63 is a different row.

The value and policy pathways
-----------------------------
The supplied propagation analysis separates critic error, conditional expectile
fitting residual, and their Bellman propagation. The fitting residual is a
conditional score expectation, not the squared loss on one observed action per
state. Frozen minibatch loss, validation loss, and finite-proposal root scores
are useful diagnostics, but none is an oracle measurement of that residual.

V2 intervention:
  1. Freeze old target-min-Q on the sampled data batch.
  2. Fit V twice on that same target/batch, advancing V's own Adam twice.
  3. Use fresh V(s_next) in r + gamma*mask*V, and project this Q target to the
     known reward/discount return interval. Fit Q once.
  4. Fit the actor once using old parameters for its targets, and preserve the
     public target EMA convention, which uses the old online Q.

In the official Gaussian AWR actor, advantage is OLD ONLINE min-Q minus OLD V.
The target critic supplies the V-regression target, not the actor advantage.
Gaussian AWR therefore gives V error an additional route into policy weights.

The unweighted BC flow and best-N policy depend instead on a behavior generator
and action rankings from learned Q. Common state-value offsets cancel in the
argmax. Action-dependent Q error, generator mismatch, and numeric sensitivity
can still damage selection even when value magnitudes remain bounded. A flow
policy trained on selected labels adds a distribution-fitting error and a
numerical integration error. Those errors need not affect return monotonically.

What is actually being compared
-------------------------------
* Gaussian IQL with/without V2: same official policy family. The complete
  gamma=.999 seed0 nine-task means are .199259 and .263704. The improvement is
  driven by Ant, where value explosion is prevented and late success rises from
  0 to .793333. Excluding Ant, the descriptive eight-task mean declines .026667.
  This supports a concrete stability benefit, not a universal residual claim.
  The matched500K component audit further shows two fits+fresh WITHOUT target
  projection at meanQ+22757, versus projection-only at meanQ-128. Full V2 is
  meanQ-143 and native is+6401. All state arrays are finite. This identifies
  projection's stability role in this one case; final ablation performance is
  still pending. See VALUE_COMPONENT_EVIDENCE.txt, not a residual-oracle claim.
  The original-discount V2 cohort is only3/9 complete, not7/9. Ant declines
  .473333->.266667, HMM improves .186667->.393333, HML improves .02->.033333.
  Two300K resumes and four unstarted tasks remain prioritized. These three
  completed tasks cannot stand in for the missing nine-task comparison.

* Finite-expectile backup: replace learned V in the Q bootstrap with the exact
  expectile of nine BC-flow target-min-Q values. Auxiliary V fits are retained
  only as clock controls. This removes learned-V fitting residual from that
  sampled backup, while leaving generator, finite-sampling and critic errors.
  Ant/CubeD/Puzzle3 full late means are .54/0/1. Other six tasks are training.
  CubeD's nearly constant wrong value shows why an algebraically small sampled
  score does not certify a useful critic.

* Mean-target finite backup: change target twin-min-Q to twin-mean-Q only.
  Keep the original min-Q deployment rule to isolate this factor. Three full
  pilots are running; partial evaluations cannot be treated as a nine-task win.
  It removes an explicit downward operation, not every source of Q bias.

* Synthetic-label rank flow: keep IQL V2 and a separate unweighted BC flow.
  Select the best of nine old-BC actions with old online min-Q; fit a separate
  policy flow to those labels using independent FM noise/time. Deploy one flow
  sample. Three full pilots are running. There are no actor V weights, Q-action
  gradients, or task-specific policy-loss coefficients.

* Real-data rank flow: an ALTERNATIVE actor objective, not an additional term on
  synthetic-label policy FM. Train on observed data actions with bounded 0/1/9
  rank weights against nine BC comparators. Under exact behavior generation,
  this has the same population best9 target; its finite estimator is sparse.
  Under actual generator misspecification the objectives differ and statewise
  weight mass can shift. Three pilots are queued. See DATA_RANK_FLOW_METHOD.txt.

* AWR mixture: another ALTERNATIVE policy family. Keep the public AWR weighted
  data log-likelihood and the same V2 Q/V controller. Replace fixed-std Gaussian
  with K5 conditional diagonal Gaussian mixture; deploy its most probable
  component mean without Q selection. Component count and std bounds are common
  across tasks. It also learns variances, so component count alone is not an
  isolated causal factor. A K1 learned-variance control is implemented but has
  no benchmark result. Three pilots are queued; see AWR_MIXTURE_METHOD.txt.

* Observed next-action anchoring: another finite-backup alternative, replacing
  one of nine generated backup actions with a verified observed next action.
  Frozen traces show weak backup shifts in the CubeD failure, so this hypothesis
  currently has lower priority. It is not added to both actor flow objectives.

* Higher expectile .99: stress pilots are queued. Nine proposals no longer
  satisfy the population best-N/expectile bound N>=tau/(1-tau), which becomes
  99. A fixed-generator finite sampled backup can still be Lipschitz; that is
  not evidence that it accurately approximates the population .99 expectile.
  The Ant/CubeD pair is now active and Puzzle3 is queued. There is a separate
  finite-operator bestN guarantee for ANY N/tau; it compares with Q_tau,N,
  not population Q_tau. See FINITE_BACKUP_POLICY_ALIGNMENT.txt for the exact
  propagated backup/extraction gap and its limits. No benchmark gain follows
  just from reducing that gap.

* Temporal critic averaging K3: average three per-snapshot twin-min predictions
  for the same BC actions. This is a separate inference recipe addressing
  ranking drift. Its selected seed0 mean .471852 did not survive independent
  confirmation: three-seed mean .452346, independent seeds1/2 mean .442593.

Two frozen-model diagnostics added in this round
------------------------------------------------
Head aggregation is a 2x2 factor comparison: target min/mean versus deployed
min/mean, with actual model/proposals held fixed within each deployment pair.
The mean-deployment rule is applied globally, never selected per task. The
min-backup group contains all nine environments; the mean-backup group has only
three pilots. Their results are not pooled. The actual CubeD300K min selector
reproduced all50 original episode arrays and32 scalar action probes exactly.

Student integration uses the same retained actor field and initial latent with
10/20/50 Euler steps. Q, V, BC and policy parameters stay fixed; the BC teacher
always remains at10 steps. Same-latent endpoint RMS is meaningful here because
the vector field and initial condition are common. Independent student/teacher
latents are instead compared by distribution distances and teacher-teacher
Monte Carlo resolution. The fifty-step solution is not an oracle ODE solution.

These diagnostics are motivated by Ant100K: the actual policy flow scored .54,
its own current best9 BC reference .10, and all50 policy episode arrays were
reproduced. The fixed-data Q diagnostic favors the reference on average, while
the student's action saturation is lower and conditional action std is similar.
Finite fitting, target history, action smoothing and Euler error are competing
explanations. No theorem that exact distillation beats a fixed teacher is being
claimed. This100K point was chosen posthoc and cannot replace the last3 metric.

The completed integration check scored .54/.40/.44 at10/20/50 steps, versus
.10 for the unchanged reference. Policy-reference energy remained about .28,
above reference-reference Monte Carlo resolution .0976. Coarse integration
affects return but does not by itself explain the observed policy/reference
gap. The original10-step episode arrays and the shared BC probe actions were
reproduced exactly. These are one-model observations, not a global decoder win.

A separate frozen-current-teacher fit advances only the policy block with its
actual extracted Adam moments. Q/V/BC, their optimizers and Bellman counters
remain fixed. Fresh training teacher samples are drawn at every auxiliary
update; cached validation labels are used only for matched diagnostics. The
policy-only artifacts cannot be loaded as a joint training resume state.
At0/256/1024 additional updates the same Ant100K model scored .54/.62/.52.
Heldout FM loss was .37276/.36074/.36368; policy-reference energy was
.28021/.24307/.24656. Thus fitting can improve local distribution metrics
without giving a monotone return improvement. The256-update peak is not a
selected algorithm, a new training seed, or a substitute for the last3 metric.

How to read causal and numerical evidence
-----------------------------------------
Frozen swaps show that a critic change alone can ruin deployed performance
while BC is held fixed, both in a late HMM case and across Puzzle3 training
seeds. They do not identify the true critic error or prove that V residual was
its sole cause. Likewise, removing learned V from a sampled backup can expose
other signed biases that previously canceled. See RESIDUAL_BIAS_INTERACTION.txt.

Even mathematically independent BC updates need not remain bitwise identical
inside different joint optimizer graphs. The actual GPU same-input32-update
probe found repeatable cross-graph BC parameter differences of3.21567e-5;
identical-graph repeats were exact. Requesting highest matmul precision did not
remove this difference in that probe. This is not proof of a hardware fault or
the cause of historical million-step divergence. Actual fixed-BC swaps, source
hashes, optimizer clocks and independent train seeds remain essential.

Evaluation and acceptance
--------------------------
The primary metric is the equal mean of CPU50 at800K/900K/1M, independently reset
with seeds42..91, then equal task and training-seed weights. Missing/stopped runs
remain missing, not zero. Early curves, a best checkpoint, auxiliary selectors,
and correlated snapshots are not additional training seeds. CPU smoke tests
and numerical probes are excluded from benchmark means.

A candidate is one common recipe across all nine tasks. Published task-category
architectures and public actor hyperparameters are inherited baselines; new
rank sample counts, fitting counts and loss coefficients are common. Complete
nine-task exploratory results must beat .47 and the fresh competitors before
independent confirmation. The current conditional promoter covers the registered
finite-min backup with min-Q selection; a different qualified recipe requires
its own frozen confirmation registration. None has qualified yet.

Training code is frozen per run, all resumed states retain source/clock/replay
identity, and primary evaluations are immutable. The two GPUs continue at eight
training runs each with XLA memory fraction .1. Auxiliary CPU jobs are reported
as diagnostics, not inflated training-run counts.
