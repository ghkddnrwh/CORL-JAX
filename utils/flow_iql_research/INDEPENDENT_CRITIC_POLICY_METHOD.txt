Independent critics and common behavior-flow proposals
======================================================

This is a separate policy-extraction candidate, using the existing learned-V
IFQLV2 value controller. It does not add finite expectile backup, boundary
supervision, GMM, rank-flow distillation or half correction.

Each base learner uses the same official-style IFQL architecture, two V Adam
fits, fresh V bootstrap and known-return target projection. Train three such
learners with independent initialization/replay seeds. Deploy one BC flow,
the first learner's, to generate nine IID action candidates. Evaluate each
candidate with each learner's minimum twin-Q head. Compare two global rules:

  mean: choose the action maximizing the mean of the three scalar Q estimates;
  median_rank: convert each critic's nine values to midranks, take the median
               rank for each action, and choose the action with largest median.

Exact ties use the first proposal, as the existing argmax selector does.
Proposal ordering is random and exchangeable; there is no new tie parameter.
Three learners and nine proposals are fixed across all nine environments.
There is no task-specific choice of a winning rule or a winning critic.

Why the rank rule is interpretable
----------------------------------
Bellman propagation can produce different state offsets and action-value
scales across approximate critics. Mean aggregation is sensitive to relative
scales. Midranks are invariant under any strictly increasing transformation
applied separately to each critic at a fixed state, including positive affine
calibration changes. This does not repair a critic's wrong within-state order.

If two of the three critics agree on the same UNIQUE best candidate, its median
rank is the maximal rank and all other candidates have a lower median rank.
The third critic can have arbitrarily large magnitudes or an incorrect order
without changing that choice. Without such agreement there is no guarantee of
the best action, oracle accuracy or improved policy return. Independently
initialized learners can still have correlated approximation errors.

The motivating evidence is the failed Pz3 seed1 confirmation: its known-goal
Q/V errors are similar in scale to successful seeds0/2, while an actual fixed
BC critic-source swap was sufficient to change performance sharply. This
motivates robustness to critic disagreement, rather than treating known-goal
error or a generic fitting loss as a universal performance proxy.

Discovery, cost and confirmation
--------------------------------
The discovery group reuses the three already observed learners0/1/2. It forms
ONE ensemble observation. It is not three independent ensemble replications.
At evaluation800K/900K/1M, every member has that many updates. The total critic
update budget is3x and the total V update budget is6x the reported per-member
step. Three base learners, including their BC fits, were trained; only the
first BC is deployed. Extra training and inference cost must be reported.

The seed0 base configuration uses32 deployment samples; this setting does not
enter its learning update. All member configs otherwise match, and every
ensemble evaluation explicitly uses9. A short CPU check verifies that sample
counts9/32 give identical base learning updates; no long-GPU bitwise claim is
made. The other BC models and all optimizer states are excluded from mixing.

Both rules are registered over all9 environments and all3 late checkpoints
before observing ensemble returns. Select the larger common9 discovery mean,
using mean aggregation on an exact tie. A discovery mean above.47 triggers
fresh-confirmation consideration; it does not achieve the research goal.
Fresh ensemble groups are fixed now to learner seeds[3,4,5] and[6,7,8], with
the first BC in each group deployed. These require54 additional base runs over
the9 tasks. The selected rule must be locked before fresh outcomes. A final
claim requires the two fresh groups' mean and the all-three-group mean both
above.47, with each task evaluated at800K/900K/1MCPU50, seeds42..91.

No existing primary scores are replaced. Parameter sets are combined only
for inference; no mixed joint-optimizer resume state is saved or trained.
