Target definitions when interpreting research logs
==================================================

For empirical_backup_core_v1, mean_empirical_backup_core_v1, and
anchored_empirical_backup_core_v1:
  td_rms is the generic learned-V snapshot Q-r-gamma*mask*V(s_next).
  V is NOT in their actual Q training target. Treat this field as a learned-V
  counterfactual, not the finite-backup Bellman residual.
  score_rms measures the auxiliary learned-V fit proxy against target minQ,
  not the finite-root score, population conditional zeta or true Q error.
  critic/critic_loss is the ACTUAL online projected finite-target loss,
  averaged over the logged replay-update window. It is not a fixed-data probe.
  backup/clip_fraction and finite_proposal_score_* are actual training-window
  finite-backup diagnostics. Zero proposal score applies only to that set.

External audit_finite_target_snapshot_v2 reconstructs the actual min/mean
finite target at immutable checkpoints, using fixed train1024 indices. Its
explicit actual_projected_finite_backup_snapshot_TD_RMS is for one frozen BC
proposal draw and current frozen target-Q, with CPU inference. It is neither
the exact original GPU batch loss nor a population Bellman error. Archived
float64-from-float32 predictions and numerical controls make this distinction
reviewable. The anchored recipe needs its own matching target reconstruction;
the min/mean auditor intentionally rejects an anchored run.

Half correction, supervised-boundary and next-success-boundary cores implement
their own probe targets and explicitly label learned-V-only counterfactuals.
For ordinary learned-V IQL/IFQL, the generic unprojected V-based snapshot is the
appropriate frozen operator residual; projection and online-batch losses remain
distinct quantities. FQL/ReBRAC runners have their own target implementations.

All raw CPU episode success scores, checkpoint identities and training targets
are unaffected by this LOG-LABEL correction. No active/resumable source was
changed. For original historical reports that list td_rms without this caveat,
use the corrected semantics and the later explicit actual-target snapshots.
