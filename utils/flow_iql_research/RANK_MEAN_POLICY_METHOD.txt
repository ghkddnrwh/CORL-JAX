Preferred discovery candidate after the complete aggregation factorial
====================================================================

Use the same three independent official-style IFQLV2 learners, tau.9/gamma999,
two V Adam fits/fresh V backup/known-return target projection, and first BC
flow's9 action proposals. Each member takes its minimum twin-Q head. Convert
each member's9 values to midranks and AVERAGE the3 rank vectors. Choose the
largest average rank, first proposal on exact ties. This replaces the median

This factorial supports the rank-coordinate transform in this discovery
cohort, and does not support adding the median reducer. Rank mean is simpler
and its discovery mean is higher. Every task uses the same rule,3 learners,N9,
tau.9 and gamma999. No environment-specific parameters or winning-rule mixture.

Mean ranks are invariant to independent strictly increasing transformations of
the critics at a state, including offsets and relative positive scales. Their
per-member contribution is bounded by the finite rank range. They do NOT have
the median's two-unique-best-critics majority guarantee; the unit counterexample
is retained. Neither rule certifies true action ranking or fixes shared errors.
Raw Q mean already ignores per-critic state offsets, so additive offsets alone
are not the reason to rank-transform its inputs.
