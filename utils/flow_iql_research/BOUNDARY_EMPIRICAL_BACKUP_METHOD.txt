Known success boundaries after removing the learned-V backup residual
====================================================================

This experiment isolates one remaining error source in the finite-expectile
backup. It does not combine rank-flow, GMM, head-mean or half correction.

Evidence motivating it
----------------------
Frozen500K Humanoid critics trained with the finite-9 expectile backup have
severe errors even on states with a known exact zero bootstrap target. Across
ALL791/338 validation success transitions, respectively, twin-Q RMS to the
known value0 is674.934/709.032. The learned-V IFQLV2 controls have7.737/4.023.
Training success-state errors are also151.535/218.377, so this is not solely a
heldout-data issue. These are boundary errors, not oracle interior-Q errors.

Uniform256-transition batches contain on average .502/.314 success examples
in the two Humanoid training datasets, and .149 in Cube-double. Puzzle4 has no
complete-success transitions at all. Counts and error audits are recorded in
analysis/terminal_boundary_audit_v1/summary.json, with actual source/checkpoint
hashes. Independent conditional-generator diagnostics further distinguish
finite proposal size, critic source and generator source. More proposals do
not restore a critic that is already nearly constant at the return floor.

Why zero values are known here
------------------------------
The installed OGBench single-task relabeler computes successes from CURRENT
state qpos/button information, sets r=0 and mask=0 at full success, and does
not condition this label on the chosen action. Locomaze single-task evaluation
uses success_timing='pre'. Therefore the implemented masked task has

    Q(s_success,a)=0 for EVERY action a.

This is a boundary condition available from training labels and benchmark
semantics, not an estimated residual or an oracle for nonterminal values.
For generic datasets whose terminal flag depends on the sampled outcome or
means a timeout, the all-action statement need not hold. This runner is scoped
to the nine verified OGBench tasks and pins the relabeler source hash.

One global intervention
------------------------
Retain the finite-9 old target-minQ expectile bootstrap, known-return target
projection, unchanged IFQL BC flow/best9 deployment, and the two auxiliary
V Adam fits used in the existing control. The learned V remains absent from
the Q backup. At every update, independently draw32 training success states.
For each state use its observed action AND one Uniform[-1,1] action. Add

    L_boundary = mean_64_pairs[ sum_two_heads Q(s_success,a)^2 ]

to the original critic loss with coefficient1. The existing joint Q/BC
optimizer takes one update on L_TD + L_boundary + L_BC. There are no extra Q,
BC or V optimizer steps. The V objective is unchanged. This specifically
tests the critic boundary condition, with no V-boundary auxiliary loss.

The coefficient1,32 states and one uniform action are fixed across all tasks.
There is no environment-specific tuning. If the training success pool is
empty, the boundary loss is exactly disabled; missing success data is never
fabricated. Thus Puzzle4 follows the unaugmented finite-backup update.

The auxiliary replay RNG is independent of the original replay RNG, stored in
the resumable checkpoint. Uniform actions use a folded JAX key and do not
consume BC matching/proposal random streams. Log main TD and boundary losses
separately, observed/uniform boundary Q RMS, actual finite-backup snapshot TD,
and boundary V RMS. Learned-V-only TD is explicitly a counterfactual metric.

Connection to residual and propagation
--------------------------------------
The finite backup already removes learned-V fitting error from bootstrapping.
Large boundary errors there demonstrate that this removal is insufficient.
Boundary errors can feed predecessor targets and propagate through Bellman
updates; direct supervision fixes a known source without diagnosing unknown
interior zeta or B_k. This does not prove that boundary error was the sole
cause, or that reducing it improves action ranking, generalization or return.

The auxiliary loss reweights the critic regression and augments actions at
known-zero states. Although the exact masked Bellman solution satisfies these
labels, a finite neural network may trade interior accuracy for boundary fit.
Therefore all pilots must finish800K/900K/1MCPU50 and compare both boundary and
interior diagnostics. Any performance claim still requires one uniform9-task
recipe and independent training-seed confirmation above FQL.47/ReBRAC.44.

Planned pilots are Humanoid-medium and Cube-double (failure cases), and
Puzzle3 (the existing finite-backup success case, to detect regressions).
No GPU result existed when this method and its validation were registered.
