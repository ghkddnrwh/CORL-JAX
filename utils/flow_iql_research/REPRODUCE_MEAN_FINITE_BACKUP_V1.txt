Actual implementation
Core: utils/flow_iql_research/mean_empirical_backup_core_v1.py
Runner: utils/flow_iql_research/train_mean_empirical_backup_v1.py
Exact finite root: utils/flow_iql_research/selection_v2.py:empirical_expectile
All active/resumable sources are frozen; preserve the original files.

At each Q update, train the behavioral flow with the ordinary unweighted
official IFQL flow-matching loss on dataset actions. Draw nine actions from
that flow at each next state, using an independent folded-in key. Evaluate
both old target-Q heads and take their arithmetic mean per candidate. Solve
the tau-expectile of those nine values exactly, then form r+gamma*mask*root
and project it to the declared return interval[-C/(1-gamma),0]. Regress both
online Q heads to that same target, update BC once, and keep the official
old-online-Q EMA convention. Deploy best9 BC candidates according to the
minimum of the online twin Q heads. There is one learner, not an ensemble
of independently trained critics, and no environment-specific winner mixture.

Shared settings
tau=.9, gamma=.999, backup/deployment candidates=9, ten Euler flow steps,
Adam .0003, batch256, EMA .005, four GELU hidden layers, Q/V LayerNorm,
no actor LayerNorm, public navigation512/manipulation256 widths.
C is the declared reward component count, not a fitted performance constant.
Other inherited public config fields are recorded verbatim in each config.json.

Important unused control
The validated runner retains two learned-V Adam fits per Q update for its
existing research clock control and diagnostics. That V does not enter this
backup or deployed action selection. Its fitting loss/legacy learned-V TD RMS
is not this finite target's residual. Do not present the extra V work as a
necessary algorithm component or silently remove it from this frozen runner.

The finite root removes the learned-V fitting approximation from this empirical
backup, not population-behavior/model/finite-sample or critic approximation
error. The twin mean removes the explicit recurrent minimum, not all bias.
Finite-min full9 scored.4, so eliminating learned-V fitting alone was insufficient
in that version. A learned-V/mean-head control is still needed to isolate the
two components. Projection and an ideal fixed-flow operator's contraction do
not guarantee stable neural optimization or improved policy ranking.
The installed library stack differs from the original paper requirements;
