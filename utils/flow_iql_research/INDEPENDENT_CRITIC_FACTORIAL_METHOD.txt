What each factor tests
----------------------
Raw mean is invariant to an independent additive state offset on each critic,
but changes under relative positive scaling of the critics. Raw pointwise
median can change action choice under independent state offsets: different
critics can become the median at different actions. Thus raw median alone is
not a general solution to critic calibration differences.

Both rank rules are invariant to a strictly increasing transformation of each
critic at a state, including positive scaling and offsets. Mean rank can still
favor the third critic's preference when the other two agree on the unique
best candidate but only weakly prefer it over a runner-up. Median rank selects
that common unique best candidate regardless of the third critic's ordering.
Unit examples exercise these differences, including ties and batched actions.

These are finite-candidate algebraic properties. They do not certify oracle
action ranks, critic accuracy, uncorrelated errors or better policy return.
The four actual nine-task means determine whether the expected factor effects
are present in this discovery cohort; interpretation must report all cells.

Implementation controls
------------------------

exactly for eight CPU keys and batched observations. Before launching the54
off-diagonal evaluations, the driver replays an actual three-critic CubeS800K
median_rank CPU50 evaluation and requires all50 successes/rewards/lengths to
match the saved version1reference exactly. No mixed learning state is saved.
Every new evaluation pins all three actual model/config/source hashes and
optimizer clocks. The strict version2 auditor requires all9x3 records per rule,
all raw episodes and all81 unique late member checkpoints; missing is not zero.
