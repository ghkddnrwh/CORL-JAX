Half correction toward a finite conditional expectile
=====================================================

This is a single intermediate intervention between two existing value recipes.
It is NOT combined with the new rank-flow or Gaussian-mixture policy families.
The coefficient is fixed at 1/2 across all environments, with no environment
specific selection. This midpoint is an ablation choice, not a proven optimum.

Keep the public IFQL behavior flow, best9 deployment, old target-Q minimum,
two fresh-V Adam fits, one Q/BC update, and known-return target projection.
At next state s', generate nine old behavior-flow actions and solve their
old target-minQ empirical expectile e_N(s') exactly. Set

    V_backup(s') = (V_new(s') + e_N(s'))/2,
    y = project_[L,0](r + gamma mask V_backup(s')).

The proposal RNG is folded into the original update key, so it does not
consume the official BC flow-matching random stream. V optimizer clocks
and BC objectives match the original V2 and full empirical-backup controls.
Production tau=.9, gamma=.999, N=9, correction=1/2 for all three pilots.

Why a partial correction is interpretable
-----------------------------------------
Fix the current target Q and proposal law. Let v_mu be the population
expectile under the behavior generator, e_V=V_new-v_mu, and e_N=e_Nsample-v_mu.
For the new backup the value displacement is exactly

    V_backup-v_mu = (e_V+e_N)/2.

Thus its contribution before projection is gamma P(e_V+E[e_N])/2. This halves
the coefficient of learned-V displacement while introducing only half the
finite-sample displacement. These errors may be correlated or cancel; the
identity does not promise lower error, return, or empirical variance. The
generator may also differ from the data behavior policy. e_V is not necessarily
the supplied theory's zeta/kappa because the critic reference and law matter.

For a fixed finite proposal set, moving from V_new to its midpoint with the
exact empirical expectile halves distance to that root. Convexity implies
nonincreasing empirical expectile objective. Its signed first-order score is
monotone, so its absolute value also cannot increase along this segment.
The score magnitude need not be exactly halved, since its slope can change.
Logs report objective and score before/after, distance, target projection and
proposal spread. These are sampled-generator diagnostics, not true residuals.

If learned V were replaced by the exact population expectile of a fixed scalar
Q, the convex combination of population and expected finite expectiles would
be monotone and 1-Lipschitz. Its Bellman operator would be a gamma contraction.
This statement does not apply to an arbitrary learned neural V, changing
generator or twin critics, and supplies no neural-training stability theorem.

The motivating empirical contrast is heterogeneous: full finite replacement
has Ant last3=.54, Cube-double=0, Puzzle3=1; existing learned-V IFQLV2 best9
has a different pattern. These are reasons to test one common midpoint, not
reasons to combine per-environment winners. Pilots must finish all three late
CPU50 evaluations. Any promising recipe still needs all nine environments
and independent training-seed confirmation against FQL=.47/ReBRAC=.44.
