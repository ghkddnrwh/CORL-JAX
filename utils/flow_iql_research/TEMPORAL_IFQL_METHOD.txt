Status: independent confirmation complete; the candidate missed the 0.47 goal.

Metric
  At each training seed and task, average CPU50 success at exactly 800K, 900K,
  and 1M Q updates. Average all nine task means equally, then training seeds
  equally. Missing tasks/checkpoints never contribute zero. The three critic
  snapshots used inside a policy are not three independent training seeds.

Common recipe
  Expectile 0.9; discount 0.999; nine behavior-flow proposals; ten Euler steps.
  Four GELU layers: published 512-wide navigation and 256-wide manipulation.
  Q/V layer normalization, no actor layer normalization, raw observations.
  Adam 3e-4, fixed learning rates. One Q and one BC-flow update per replay batch.
  Two V Adam fits use the same batch and the frozen old minimum target-Q.
  The V optimizer owns its own moments and clock. After 1M Q/BC updates it has
  made 2M updates. Use the newly fitted V in the critic backup and clip that
  target to [-n/(1-discount),0], with n the known reward-component count.
  Preserve the official target-Q EMA convention: its rate is 0.005 and its
  source is the old online Q. The BC flow uses plain flow matching, without
  an advantage weight or an action gradient from Q.

Deployment
  Retain online critic parameter snapshots every 100K updates. Use the current
  BC flow to generate nine actions from Gaussian latents. For each action take
  the minimum twin-Q prediction within each critic, then average these values
  across the three most recent past/current critic snapshots. Choose the action
  with the largest average. Do not average success scores or select a checkpoint
  separately for each task.

  At 800K: critics 600K,700K,800K; actor 800K.
  At 900K: critics 700K,800K,900K; actor 900K.
  At 1M:   critics 800K,900K,1M;   actor 1M.

Rationale and limits
  Extra V fits address regression underfitting; target projection uses known
  return bounds; a behavioral flow supplies a richer action proposal family.
  Temporal Q averaging tests sensitivity to changing action rankings. A frozen
  HMM intervention with identical BC900 achieved 0.70 using Q800 and 0.02 using
  Q900. This establishes a critic-source effect in that intervention, not a true
  critic-bias estimate or a proof that the expectile residual caused it.

  Minibatch expectile loss and generated-action residuals are not oracle
  conditional residuals. Repeated fitting need not help heldout states. The
  temporal filter changes policy extraction only; it does not make the neural
  Bellman training operator a contraction. Native-vs-V2 BC parameters can differ
  despite equal replay/JAX RNG states, so fixed-BC causal controls are separate.
  No novelty claim or all-task/all-expectile stability guarantee is made here.
