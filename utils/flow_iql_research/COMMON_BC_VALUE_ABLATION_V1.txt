Common actual behavior policy for native versus corrected IQL critics
Registered before any new crossed outcome. One exploratory seed0, nine tasks.

Question
The native IFQL and V2 IFQL behavior flow parameters and Adam moments are not
bitwise equal at the retained checkpoints, despite identical configurations,
agent RNGs, and replay RNGs. Their own-policy difference therefore cannot be
attributed solely to the value-update bundle. The origin of the BC differences
has not been diagnosed. Equal terminal RNG states do not prove equal arrays or
equal compiled numerical trajectories throughout training.

Fixed protocol
Use the already retained native and V2 checkpoints at 800K, 900K, and 1M,
all tasks, seed0. No training or GPU jobs. Freeze the actual V2 behavior-flow
parameters, moments, and action RNG at each checkpoint. Compare native Q with
this common V2 BC against existing V2 Q with exactly that same V2 BC. Use max
of twin-min Q across nine flow proposals, 50 CPU episodes, reset seeds42..91.
All scores use equal means across three checkpoints and all nine tasks.
No winning-checkpoint or per-task recipe mixtures and no missing-as-zero.

Validation gate
First reproduce V2 self-compositions on Ant/Humanoid-medium/Puzzle-3x3 at1M.
Require equality of the whole dynamic state and all 50 success/reward/length
arrays against retained V2 N9 evaluations. Only after all three pass may the
27 native-Q/common-V2-BC evaluations start. Check source/config/checkpoint SHA,
finite state and the actual Adam counts (native V1M, V2 separate V2M at1M).
Use the existing frozen factorized-policy V3 evaluator without editing any
checkpoint, primary evaluation, training source or optimizer trajectory.

Interpretation
V2 Q/common V2 BC minus native Q/common V2 BC is a value-bundle contrast
conditional on this specific common BC source. It does not separately identify
extra V fitting, fresh bootstrap order or target projection, nor imply a
universal value improvement. Common-BC native Q minus native-own-BC native Q
is a policy-extraction contrast conditional on the native critic.
Both contrasts are secondary exploratory ablations. They do not provide
independent training confirmation, qualify a newly selected policy or complete
the goal. They cannot establish why the native BC weights differed.
